Commissioned paintings

Your story, in oil

A personal oil painting, made to measure in the studio on the Bloemgracht. A cityscape or your front door, a still life of things that matter, or a portrait of someone you love.

Start your commission

From idea to canvas

  1. You tell meWe start with your story, memory or favourite place.
  2. A plan togetherWe decide on composition, size and mood.
  3. GatheringObjects and images, or we visit that one place together.
  4. Sketches and photosI capture what makes the story complete.
  5. PaintingBy hand, in oil, in my studio.

Portraits too

A loved one, a child, a parent or the dog who is part of the family. A painted portrait is more intimate than any photo and stays in the family for generations. From a cherished photo, or with the sitter in front of me. Both work.
